About NSP

Nitya Stotra Parayanam (NSP) is committed to preserving and sharing the rich heritage of Vedic literature. Our team consists of dedicated and expert Gurus and devotees who conduct free online classes, guiding participants through the recitation and understanding of various stotras. We believe in the transformative power of these sacred hymns to bring peace and enlightenment to individuals and communities. In line with our principle of 'Manave Seve Madhava Seva ,' all donations are used for charitable initiatives, reflecting our commitment to selfless service.
